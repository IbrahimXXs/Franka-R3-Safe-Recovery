Reading the measurements
Forces are fixture-on-tool forces in world coordinates. Positive Fz points upward: insertion resistance is max(Fz, 0), and retreat resistance is max(−Fz, 0). Torque is measured about the peg center.
Recovery work is a contact-resisting work proxy, ∫ max(0, −(F·v + τ·ω)) dt. It is not motor energy. Realignment is included in recovery time and work.
Blank values mean unavailable or inapplicable, not zero. The overlap screen is a numerical review flag; passing it does not establish convergence. Force and torque budgets classify results after execution and are not enforced controller limits.
